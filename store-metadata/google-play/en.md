## Full description (up to 4000 chars)
KORA is a simple and convenient app for building your family (genealogy) tree. It works fully offline: no registration, no accounts, no cloud — all data is stored only on your device.

Features:
• Multiple family trees in a single app
• Add relatives: first name, last name, dates of birth and death
• Relationships between people: parents and spouses
• A photo and a photo gallery for each person (from your library)
• Notes about each person with Markdown formatting
• Visual tree with connections: drag the cards and zoom in and out
• Merge trees and extract a separate family into a new tree
• Import and export your data for backup or transfer between devices
• Light and dark themes
• Russian and English languages

Privacy:
• No registration and no accounts
• Data is never sent to any server, no cloud is used
• No ads; your family data never leaves your device (usage analytics only)
• Open source under the MIT License

KORA — your roots, always with you.
